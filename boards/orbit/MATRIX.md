## DUAL-SOLDER LEADS — the front-side bench work list

**1 leads** must be soldered on the FRONT face as well as the back.  Each one is real copper the routed board depends on: there is no plating on a milled board, so an unsoldered lead here is an open circuit, not a cosmetic miss.

> **Seat every LED FLUSH and solder it on the BACK only.** No LED lead is a layer bridge on this board. Operator ruling, 2026-08-01: *"seating an LED 1.5mm proud for an under-flange front joint is EXTREMELY annoying by hand — ten promoted LED anodes is unacceptable. A bare wire via (open access, both faces) is jumper-class and fine. Therefore: LED leads may no longer be layer bridges."* The ten front joints this board used to ask for are PRE-SEEDED CROSSINGS in the via ledger above — same layer change, made with a threaded wire in open board instead of an iron tip under an LED flange.

## Power pads — READ BEFORE WIRING

**PAD1 is `+` and is the RIGHT-HAND pad (x 16.0); PAD2 is `-` and is the LEFT-HAND pad (x 10.0).** This TRANSPOSES the layout note in SPEC, and the silk `+`/`-` legend follows the copper, not the note. The transposition is forced, not cosmetic: the VBAT corridor runs rightward from the `+` pad to SW1 and needs 2.45 mm of vertical room under SW1's blade, which the strip only has on that side.

Reversing them puts the battery backwards across Q1 and C1.
