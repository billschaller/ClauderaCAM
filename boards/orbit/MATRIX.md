## Charlieplex ring

## BENCH JUMPERS — wires the COPPER does not make

**2 jumper(s).**  Same 22 AWG solid wire as the stitched vias (Board A shipped seven of these).  This is not a repair and not an oversight: after the side-2 scrub growth the board is exactly one routable connection short, and WHICH connection is short moves with the seed set (four sets measured, four different nets).  So the shortfall was spent deliberately, on the pair of terminals that is easiest to solder.

- **L2** — solder a 24.5 mm wire from **LED11-2** (42.630, 30.000) to **TP3-1** (44.000, 5.540).
  Both pads are on the **back** face, so the wire lies flat there for its whole run and crosses no front component.  `LED11-2` is an LED anode ring — the joint you already solder that lead into, open on the back; `TP3-1` is a bare Ø1.8 ISP pad, designed to be touched.  Neither end is an SMD land.
  Until it is soldered, `L2` is OPEN — the board is not finished without it.
- **L3** — solder a 27.4 mm wire from **LED12-2** (40.134, 39.315) to **S1-1B** (60.750, 21.250).
  Both pads are on the **back** face, so the wire lies flat there for its whole run and crosses no front component.  `LED12-2` is an LED anode ring, open on the back; `S1-1B` is a tactile-switch leg ring — a through hole you solder anyway.  Neither end is an SMD land.
  Until it is soldered, `L3` is OPEN — the board is not finished without it.

## DUAL-SOLDER LEADS — the front-side bench work list

**1 leads** must be soldered on the FRONT face as well as the back.  Each one is real copper the routed board depends on: there is no plating on a milled board, so an unsoldered lead here is an open circuit, not a cosmetic miss.

> **Seat every LED FLUSH and solder it on the BACK only.** No LED lead is a layer bridge on this board. Operator ruling, 2026-08-01: *"seating an LED 1.5mm proud for an under-flange front joint is EXTREMELY annoying by hand — ten promoted LED anodes is unacceptable. A bare wire via (open access, both faces) is jumper-class and fine. Therefore: LED leads may no longer be layer bridges."* The ten front joints this board used to ask for are PRE-SEEDED CROSSINGS in the via ledger above — same layer change, made with a threaded wire in open board instead of an iron tip under an LED flange.

## Pour census — every copper region and what makes it live

MEASURED on the exported gerbers with gerbv (the rasterizer that shares no code with this generator), not asserted from the model.  A region with no conductor is not automatically a defect: on a milled board with no plating, a THT lead's FRONT ring belongs to no net by construction (the R3 finding), and the flip gauges are deliberately dead copper you read with a loupe.  What matters is that every region is either LIVE or dead ON PURPOSE.

## Silk: refs deliberately NOT printed

Silk is this board's congestion canary, not decoration: a label that cannot be seated cleanly means the copper under it is too tight, and the answer is to un-compress the copper. Every seat on this board now clears the 0.30 ink-to-pad law by at least 33%, so the refs below are dropped for a different reason — REDUNDANCY, not crowding.

- **TP3** — the ISP block prints the FUNCTIONAL legend `SCK` at this pad instead. A bench looking for SCK reads `SCK`; `TP3` would be a second name for the same hole and buys the operator nothing.
- **TP5** — the ISP block prints the FUNCTIONAL legend `RST` at this pad instead. A bench looking for RST reads `RST`; `TP5` would be a second name for the same hole and buys the operator nothing.
- **TP6** — the ISP block prints the FUNCTIONAL legend `GND` at this pad instead. A bench looking for GND reads `GND`; `TP6` would be a second name for the same hole and buys the operator nothing.

## Power pads — READ BEFORE WIRING

**PAD1 is `+` and is the RIGHT-HAND pad (x 16.0); PAD2 is `-` and is the LEFT-HAND pad (x 10.0).** This TRANSPOSES the layout note in SPEC, and the silk `+`/`-` legend follows the copper, not the note. The transposition is forced, not cosmetic: the VBAT corridor runs rightward from the `+` pad to SW1 and needs 2.45 mm of vertical room under SW1's blade, which the strip only has on that side.

Reversing them puts the battery backwards across Q1 and C1.
